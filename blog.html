Entry 168, Blog post 3: October 2026

On Early Releases, On Audio Progress, On Bookstores

  • 10/3/26 at 9:47AM

On Early Releases

A month away! I will be releasing my physical book a month early, to coincide with some promotional ARCs that I have secured. Helps me to get some reviews. Shhh, don’t tell anyone. It is both a stress relief and a stressor to me in this part of the process. I am now pretty hands off, and the work has been completed, but it is hard when I have been so hands on, especially in the last couple months. That loss of ‘control’ in a way because there is no more work to do bothers me. I’ve been channeling that into writing the next book, but also I don’t want to get my wires crossed until Crimson Copper Bluebird is out, and I definitely don’t want CCB to suffer in any way because I am not ‘babying’ it. It’s my oldest, but it still needs love. Haha.

On Audio Progress

Audiobook is progressing well. I am so excited to share! I’ll wait until release to give more details, but the person bringing my book to life is phenomenal.

On Bookstores

For my Charlotte locals, I am working on something pretty cool! I am going to try and get CCB in a physical store… don’t hold your breath but do get excited. I’ve had some thoughts about book signings and stickers and stuff like that, but I think it will need to wait until after release.

P. S. I think I might start talking about the books I am reading? That being said I'm not one to review books or really share my opinion on them unless they move me. I also read extremely slow. I will call out I read I Who Have Never Known Men early this year and I think that has been the peak for me. I also read The Count of Monte Cristo to much enjoyment. I read them both back to back, and I don't think I've ever read two books in a row that moved me so completely before. Currently, I just finished Into the Blue and Witchcraft for Wayward Girls, and I have moved on to Flowers for Algernon and Final Girl Support Group. I believe I read Flowers as a short story, but I don't think I have read this 'longer' version. I usually have two books going in tandem, but again, I am a slow reader so I'm not hitting like twenty-four books a year or anything like that. I think actually I am at thirteen this year, and the only reason I am doing that is because I joined a book club and separate from my books read I have only needed to re-read Crimson Copper Bluebird once (or twice) this year. I'd like to get more Pratchett (and Pratchett-esque) in the mix, send me your recommendations!

Until Next Month,

J. P. Tynan

Entry 167, Blog post 2: September 2026

On Physical Releases, On Audiobooks, On Potential Future Plans

  • 9/1/26 at 5:22PM

On Physical Releases

Release date has been set for November 10th!! Preorders for Crimson Copper Bluebird are live, with the physical release well underway. I had a bit of a weird thought setting up my paperback: books are too big to keep to yourself, they are meant to be shared. That doesn’t guarantee they are meant to be universally enjoyed, but I do feel like it is a disservice to not share your story with someone if you have it. Whenever I go to a bookstore (lookin’ at you Park Road) or a pop up or a library I always get this weird pull like all the books are asking to be heard. Some are pushy, some are quiet, but all of them are calling out to me. I just wish I had more time to listen. As a side note I read about everything and I’ll read about anything, so if you have a recommendation send it my way and chances are I’ll give it a look.

On Audiobooks

Speaking of releases, I’ve got an audiobook in the works!! So excited to share it as more details come out, but it’s looking like it will be primarily on Audible to start!

On Potential Future Plans

I’m a big future planner, maybe strategist is a better word? I’ve been debating what my next book should be, be it a continuation in the Crimson Copper Bluebird universe, a standalone, or another series start. All will come out in due time, but I guess what I’m unsure about is the order. I keep my progress on my website to keep myself honest, but sometimes I wonder if that is dishonest. Like I am around 30% done with Book XYZ, but it might stay that way for six months while I work on Book ABC. Another thing is I am waiting to get some feedback on Crimson Copper Bluebird to see if the demand for a sequel outweighs the interest in diversifying my catalog. I will say the initial feedback I’ve received has been so heartwarming, and really encourages me to keep at it. Everyone’s gotta start somewhere, and at the end of the day, I’m proud of what I’ve been able to share.

P. S. I want to get ahead of this now, I happen to have posted this early, I don't actually plan on always hitting the first of the month. Just hitting within the month is my goal.

Until Next Month,

J. P. Tynan

Entry 166, Blog post 1: August 2026

On Beginnings, Debuts, and Timelines

  • 8/1/26 at 4:58PM

On Beginnings

Hello and welcome. This will be the first blog post I make public, so apologies in advance if it comes off stiff, I’m still figuring everything out. My goal here is to give monthly musings/updates/progress as it relates to my writing career. This will also be a crosspost from my personal journal, and separate from my quarterly updates in my newsletter. Some ground rules, in my personal journal I typically write about my life, the present, and some other third thing. Again, here my goal is to be a bit more focused on the writing aspect of my life, but with how consuming it is I’m sure some other stuff will slip through haha. My goal is to never monetize or run ads or sell anything like that on here, so if you’re just looking for the rambling of a fledgling author you are in the right place lol. I will surely endorse stuff or talk about other media to be clear, but it will never be because I received money or at least not without being completely transparent. Again, I hate ads in the middle of personal chats, so I’m gonna keep this as ‘real’ as humanly possible. I will also use this as a spot to respond to any interesting feedback I receive, so send it my way.

On Debuts

In terms of updates, my debut novel, Crimson Copper Bluebird, is well on its way for a Fall 2026 release. I thought the writing was the hardest part until I got to everything after hahaha. To be completely transparent I am so ready to write the next one! All the little steps from finished manuscript to published book can feel a bit overwhelming at times. Thankfully I have wonderful friends and collaborators to drag me kicking and screaming across the finish line. A bit behind the scenes, my long term goal is to come out with at minimum seven books, and then to evaluate next steps from there. I’m a bit nervous about getting pigeonholed into a single genre, but I think fantasy is a good base for me. At least it is what I am most interested in writing about.

On Timelines

I guess it is worth laying out the timeline that it took me to get here. I’d say I’d had the idea for Crimson Copper Bluebird a bit over a decade ago, along with several other book ideas. I’m sure I will get into inspirations in later posts but suffice to say all my book ideas start out as weird dreams. All of them sat at around 10 to 50 pages written each, with me only incrementing about 10 pages a year or slowly adding outlines. After grad school (2021) I focused on getting licensed (2023) because I wanted to make sure my career was stable so I could support myself. It was 2024 that I finally felt ready, and so I picked what I believed was the most fleshed out idea and then built it out into what is now Crimson Copper Bluebird. My goal was to write about 10 pages a week until the story was complete, and then cut 500 words a week until I had under 120k words, and then read through and edit around seven times. I ended up formally finishing all re-writes and edits in April of this year, so now the last couple months have only been focused on getting it out there. I have so many fun facts or weird tangential stuff I want to share, but it’ll have to be saved for the future.

Until Next Month,

J. P. Tynan
